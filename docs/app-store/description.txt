FTC HUB streamlines everything your FIRST Tech Challenge team needs during competition season. Manage rosters, plan events, track inventory, scout matches, and mirror the official FTC scoring system from a single app.

FTC HUB is built by mentors for mentors. Keep your team synchronized, compliant, and ready to compete.
